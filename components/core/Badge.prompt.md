Small status chip for festivals, solar terms, and holiday/rest-day flags.

```jsx
<Badge tone="brand" solid>休</Badge>
<Badge tone="gold">端午节</Badge>
<Badge tone="good">宜出行</Badge>
<Badge tone="info">夏至</Badge>
```

- `tone`: `brand` · `gold` · `good` (宜) · `avoid` (忌) · `info` (节气) · `neutral`
- `solid` for filled emphasis (e.g. the 休/班 rest-day flag).
